# Contributing

Pull requests are welcome for: typo fixes, clearer wording, better examples,
new market notes (we are Australian; UK, NZ, US notes are gladly taken), and
new skills that follow the same shape.

## Shape of a skill

`skills/<slug>/SKILL.md` with frontmatter `name`,
`description`, `when_to_use`, `metadata.category` (Prepare, Decide, Write,
Detect), then the doctrine, the steps, the output template, the checklist.
Every skill ends with the same two lines. Keep them.

## What we will not merge

- Anything that invents facts, quotes or journalists.
- Scoring systems, rubrics or "attention" models. The skills are craft you
  apply by hand to one story. The scoring lives in Gust PR and is not open.
- Contact lists or journalist data of any kind.
- Market-specific claims without a source.

## Where the content comes from

Skills are exported one way from the private Gust PR playbook. If you want a
change to stick across releases, send the PR here and we will fold it back.
