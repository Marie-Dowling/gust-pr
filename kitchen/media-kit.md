Fill this in once. Every Gust PR skill and agent reads it first, so you are
never asked the same question twice. Copy it to `media-kit.md` in the folder
where you work, or let `media-kit-setup` build it with you.

Rules: only true things. Leave a line blank rather than guess. Anything
marked `[needed]` is a gap the Editor in Chief will chase before a pitch.

## T&C gate

Everything shared with a journalist can, and probably will, be used in the
media, publicly and never anonymised. By dating this line you accept that
(1) what you share is true, (2) you are not making claims about other people
or companies you cannot back, and (3) nothing said to a journalist is off
the record.
